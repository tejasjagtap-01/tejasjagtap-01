#### 🏎️ [Formula 1 Multi-Era Telemetry & Strategic Intelligence Engine](https://github.com/tejasjagtap-01)
*An end-to-end ETL and business intelligence pipeline transforming 70+ years of Grand Prix telemetry into driver racecraft metrics and reliability risk indexes.*

- **Relational Analytics:** Authored PostgreSQL window queries evaluating circuit pole-to-win conversion rates (identifying 80%+ qualifying-critical tracks like COTA and Abu Dhabi) and isolating Sunday driver racecraft deltas.
- **Executive Delivery:** Built a multi-era Power BI dashboard auditing round-by-round race classifications, constructor dominance cycles, and drivetrain failure modes.

#### ⚡ [Zepto Quick-Commerce Inventory Optimization & Revenue Leakage](https://github.com/tejasjagtap-01)
*A supply chain analytics framework simulating ultra-fast delivery catalog ergonomics, dark-store fulfillment paths, and stockout leakage.*
- **SQL Data Auditing:** Applied advanced CTEs, `DENSE_RANK()`, and conditional aggregation in PostgreSQL to identify high-ticket out-of-stock SKUs directly damaging daily GMV.
- **Unit Economics & Logistics:** Computed price-per-gram metrics across consumer packaging tiers and calculated cumulative category weight loads to optimize warehouse pick-and-pack fulfillment ergonomics.
- **Dashboarding:** Paired SQL layers with Power BI dashboards tracking margin subsidization, category discount spreads, and real-time inventory risks.
